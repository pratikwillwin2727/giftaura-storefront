Privacy-First Architecture

GiftAura is engineered specifically to protect the business owner's personal details:

  • Personal phone number, WhatsApp number, and UPI ID are never rendered in frontend code or templates.
  • Customer interactions route privately through on-site COD checkout and official Instagram @gifts.aura_.
  • All data persists in safe local storage with optional zero-backend email webhook connectivity (Web3Forms).
